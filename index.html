Our Story

Made with intention, crafted with love

Bloom Stone is a Malaysia-based brand dedicated to creating handcrafted home décor that blends simplicity with thoughtful design. Each piece is carefully made by hand from start to finish, ensuring that no two items are ever exactly alike.

We use non-toxic, eco-friendly materials and sealers which makes our product water-resistant and safe for everyday use while maintaining durability and quality.

At Bloom Stone, we believe that beautiful home décor should be both accessible and meaningful.
